# Business Strategy Recommendations

*Report generated 2026-09-06. Recommendations are derived directly from the segment profiles in `segmentation_report.md` — each strategy is only proposed where the corresponding segment profile supports it.*

## High-Value Loyal

**Profile:** 3,816 customers (36.34% of the base) contributing 83.3% of total spend. Average recency 30 days, frequency 21.8 orders, monetary value £1,347.58, tenure 18.5 months.

**Strategy:** **Retain & reward.** These customers already generate the majority of revenue relative to their size. Priorities: loyalty-tier perks, early access to new ranges, premium/concierge service, and proactive relationship management to protect this base from competitor poaching.

## Emerging Customers

**Profile:** 2,228 customers (21.22% of the base) contributing 8.4% of total spend. Average recency 27 days, frequency 5.9 orders, monetary value £232.86, tenure 5.7 months.

**Strategy:** **Growth & cross-sell.** Shorter tenure but healthy recent activity. Priorities: category expansion recommendations, loyalty-programme enrolment, and frequency-building nudges (e.g. subscribe-and-save) to convert them toward the High-Value Loyal segment.

## At-Risk / Lapsing

**Profile:** 2,992 customers (28.5% of the base) contributing 8.23% of total spend. Average recency 127 days, frequency 4.8 orders, monetary value £169.77, tenure 13.9 months.

**Strategy:** **Re-engagement campaigns.** Longer-tenured customers whose recency has slipped. Priorities: win-back offers, a satisfaction/service check-in, and lightweight surveys to diagnose why engagement dropped before assuming price sensitivity.

## New / Low Engagement

**Profile:** 1,464 customers (13.94% of the base) contributing 0.07% of total spend. Average recency 105 days, frequency 0.2 orders, monetary value £2.95, tenure 3.9 months.

**Strategy:** **Onboarding.** Recently acquired with minimal purchase history. Priorities: a structured welcome journey, first/second-purchase incentives, and early signal-based triage (customers who don't convert within a defined window should move into a distinct reactivation track).

## Prioritisation

Given that **High-Value Loyal** alone represents 83.3% of total spend from 36.34% of customers, retention investment for this segment should be prioritised first — the revenue risk of losing even a small fraction of this group outweighs acquisition-focused spend on smaller segments. The overall base shows 63.01% of total spend concentrated in the top 20% of customers by value, reinforcing a Pareto-style prioritisation of retention over broad-based acquisition.
